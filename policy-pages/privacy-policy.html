Learning & Development · Certificate Verification (test app)

Privacy Policy

This bot is a personal development test, not a product. Here's exactly what it does with what you send it.

1

What this is

A one-person test of a certificate-verification workflow, run through Microsoft Teams for personal development purposes. It is not a commercial product, not deployed for production use, and not offered to the public.

2

What's collected

Only what you type into the chat: a name, a work email address, a certification name, and a public credential link (for example, a Credly badge URL). Nothing is collected passively or from any other source.

3

How it's used

Those fields are sent to the developer's verification backend, which checks the credential against its public badge page and returns a verified or needs review result in the same chat. That's the only processing that happens.

4

Where it's stored

In a local development database on the developer's own machine, for as long as the test environment is running. It isn't backed up, replicated, or synced anywhere else.

5

Who sees it

Nobody but you and the developer running this test. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

6

Deletion

Test data is discarded when the environment is torn down. To ask for earlier removal, use the contact below.
